Methodology

Tennis Analytics computes surface-aware Elo ratings and betting-market analysis for ATP men's singles, deterministically from public raw data. Every number is reproducible from source.

Ratings

Elo with a configurable, experience-decaying K-factor. Separate ladders for overall and each surface (clay / hard / grass) — a player's clay rating updates only on clay. Win expectation is recorded before each match, so ratings never peek at the result. Rankings show active players (last 12 months) with a minimum match count to avoid small-sample distortion.

Market analysis

De-margined implied probabilities from closing odds (Pinnacle, with Bet365 and market-max). We surface average market error (where the market mis-prices a player), Elo-vs-market divergence, and calibration (Brier score). Informational only — not betting advice.

Data lineage

  • Matches, rankings, players — Jeff Sackmann tennis_atp (public).
  • Closing odds — tennis-data.co.uk.

Limitations

Odds are a single closing snapshot per book (no intraday line movement). Match dates approximate tournament start. Player-name joins across sources use a last-name + initial key. Cold-start Elo is unreliable below ~20 matches.
